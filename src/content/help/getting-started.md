Welcome. This page covers signing in, finding your tools, your profile, and where to get help.

## Signing in

- You join by **invitation**. An admin invites you, and you get an email with a link to set your password. The link expires (72 hours by default). If it has expired, ask the admin to resend it.
- Sign in with your work email and password. After several wrong passwords in a row (5 by default) the account is locked for a short time (15 minutes by default). An admin can unlock it sooner.
- **Forgot your password?** Click *Forgot password* on the sign-in page. A reset link is emailed to you and is valid for an hour.

## The dashboard and the sidebar

After you sign in you land on the **Dashboard**. It shows a card for each tool you can use.

![The dashboard shows only the tools your role gives you](images/dashboard.jpg)

The sidebar on the left has:

- **Tools**: the tools your role (or a per-user grant) gives you. If you expect a tool and don't see it, ask an admin.
- **Admin**: only if you have an admin permission (users, roles, tools, access log, backups, settings).
- **Support → Help & Docs**: this help section.
- At the bottom: your name and role, **My Profile** and **Sign out**. The arrow collapses the sidebar.

## My Profile

Click the person icon at the bottom of the sidebar.

![My Profile](images/profile.jpg)

- **Name**: change how your name appears.
- **Change password**: at least 10 characters, with letters and numbers. You need your current password.
- **Salsify API key** (only if you use the Shipping Rates Generator or the Packaging Configurator): your personal key for product lookups, and for Salsify syncs and pushes you start yourself. It's encrypted and never shown again; only the last 4 characters are displayed.

## Help & Docs

**Help & Docs** shows only the guides for the tools and admin areas **you** can access, so what you see may differ from a colleague. Tools also have a **Help** link next to their tabs that opens the right guide for you.

![Help & Docs](images/help-index.jpg)

Use **Print / Save as PDF** on any guide for a paper copy.

## Getting help

- A tool doesn't work as described: tell the tool's owner (for example the EDI Admin for Automated Emails) or a platform admin, with the time, the page and the exact error message.
- Access problems (a missing tool, a locked account, an expired invitation): ask a platform admin.
- Everything you do in the portal (sign-ins, changes, denied pages) is recorded in an access log that admins can review.
